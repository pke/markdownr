Privacy Policy

Markdownr — Markdown Viewer

Short version

Markdownr does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device.

Data collection

Markdownr does not collect any data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs that gather information. The app does not require an internet connection and makes no network requests.

File access

Markdownr reads Markdown files that you explicitly open through the system file picker or by sharing a file from another app. These files are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or transmitted anywhere.

Third-party services

Markdownr does not use any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Children's privacy

Because Markdownr collects no data whatsoever, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL. Since there is no data collection, there is no way to notify users directly — but the commitment to privacy will remain.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us at markdownr@dudesoft.app.
